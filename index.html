Wake up to light coming off the water. Fall River Retreat sits on a wooded lot with its own dock, a short walk from town, and far enough away that evenings are quiet.

Inside, the main floor is open and bright: a stone fireplace in the living room, a full kitchen with room for everyone, and a long dining table that seats ten. Upstairs, four bedrooms keep families and friends comfortably apart.

Outside, the screened porch is the best seat in the house. Bring a book, a coffee, and nowhere to be.
